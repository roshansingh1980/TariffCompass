# TariffCompass — Launch Strategy

_Last updated: 28 August 2026_

This document governs the **one-time concentrated Beta/V1 launch**. It is intentionally different from [`MARKETING_STRATEGY.md`](MARKETING_STRATEGY.md), which governs ongoing acquisition after launch.

The launch is not a passive website release. It is a coordinated founder-led campaign designed to create immediate paid traction, customer interviews, testimonials, and proof that TariffCompass is worth **C$99/month for Business** and **C$249–250/month for Advisor**.

## Launch objective

### Immediate launch target

- **10 paying Business customers**
- **1 paying Advisor customer**

### Excellent launch outcome

- **10 paying Business customers**
- **3 paying Advisor customers**

### Phase-1 commercial milestone

- **30 paying Business customers**
- **3 paying Advisor customers**

This is the first meaningful traction threshold. The launch exists to accelerate the path to it.

## Governing principle

> **Do not buy broad awareness. Borrow concentrated, trusted audiences that already have the tariff problem — and arrive with meetings booked.**

The event is not the acquisition engine. The event is the **credibility, concentration, urgency and deadline mechanism** around a founder-led sales campaign.

# 1. Geographic launch focus — Ontario first

Ontario is the preferred first launch market because it provides a dense concentration of:

- manufacturers and exporters;
- auto/parts and advanced manufacturing businesses;
- U.S.-exposed SMEs;
- import-dependent distributors and wholesalers;
- regional accounting/advisory firms;
- trade/economic-development organizations.

The product remains Canada-wide and bilingual-ready. Ontario is simply the first concentrated acquisition theatre.

### Priority Ontario clusters

1. **Windsor / Essex** — automotive, parts, cross-border U.S. exposure.
2. **Kitchener-Waterloo / Cambridge / Guelph** — advanced manufacturing and exporters.
3. **Brampton / Mississauga / GTA west** — manufacturing, distribution, import/export concentration.
4. **London** — diversified manufacturing and regional SMEs.
5. **Niagara / Hamilton** — manufacturing plus accountant/advisor access.

Do not attempt a simultaneous national field launch.

# 4. Pre-launch acquisition — meetings before foot traffic

The launch must not rely on people discovering the booth or website.

## Business pre-launch list

Build a named list of approximately **100–150 Ontario manufacturers / importers / exporters** concentrated around selected launch cities.

Prioritize companies with:

- meaningful U.S. trade exposure;
- automotive / machinery / steel / aluminum / furniture / food / chemicals / electronics exposure where the product has strong coverage;
- identifiable owner, CFO, controller, VP Finance, operations or trade decision-maker;
- enough trade volume that C$99/month is economically trivial if the platform is useful.

### Founder outreach message

The core offer is not "try our tariff website."

It is:

> **Give me one product you import/export, the HS code if you know it, and an approximate annual trade value. I will show you your exposure and what TariffCompass would monitor for you.**

The event gives the outreach urgency and legitimacy:

> "We are launching TariffCompass around the CME Ontario Manufacturing Outlook events and are opening the first 10 Founding Business memberships."

### Pre-booking target

Before the Business launch event(s), aim for:

- **20–30 booked Business demos / exposure assessments**;
- at least **10 high-intent prospects** who understand the Founding Business offer;
- a calendar full enough that event walk-up traffic is incremental, not essential.

## Advisor pre-launch list

Build a named list of approximately **30–50 regional accounting / fractional-CFO / SME advisory firms**, particularly firms serving manufacturers, distributors and import/export clients.

Pre-book conversations before the CPA Ontario event.

### Advisor offer

> **TariffCompass shows you which trade-exposed clients need attention, what changed, the approximate financial exposure, and the official source — without turning your firm into a customs brokerage.**

### Pre-booking target

Before the Advisor launch event:

- **8–12 booked Advisor demos**;
- enough qualified conversations to credibly recruit the first **1–3 founding Advisor firms**.

# 5. Founding launch offers

## Founding Business — first 10 only

- Standard price: **C$99/month**
- Founding price: **50% off for the first 12 months**
- Then automatically renews at standard pricing, subject to then-current terms
- Hard cap: **10 accounts**

Founding-customer benefits may include:

- direct founder onboarding;
- one exposure-mapping setup session;
- direct product-feedback channel;
- early access to launch-critical improvements;
- Founding Customer designation where useful.

Do not promise lifetime discounts or unlimited bespoke consulting.

## Founding Advisor — first 3 only

- Standard price: **C$249–250/month**
- Founding price: **50% off for the first 12 months**
- Hard cap: **3 accounts**

Founding Advisor benefits should center on onboarding their initial client portfolio and direct product feedback.

## Scarcity

The founding limits must be genuine. The website/demo may show remaining availability, e.g.:

> **7 of 10 Founding Business memberships remaining**

Do not manufacture false scarcity.

# 6. Live demo mechanics

The live demo should be **90–120 seconds before switching to the prospect's own situation**.

## Business sequence

1. Introduce Bob in one sentence.
2. Show his saved product / HS exposure and markets.
3. Show a real sourced change or current treatment.
4. Show estimated dollar impact.
5. Show source / effective date / confidence.
6. Show monitoring / alerts / upcoming dates.
7. Ask the prospect for one product / HS code / annual trade value.
8. Run or schedule their own exposure analysis.

## Advisor sequence

1. Introduce Sarah in one sentence.
2. Show Client Exposure Radar.
3. Show which clients require attention.
4. Drill into one client and one saved exposure.
5. Show what changed, financial impact and source.
6. Show client-ready output.
7. Ask the accountant how many clients import/export materially.
8. Offer to map 3–5 example clients during onboarding.

Do not give a 20-minute product tour before demonstrating economic value.

# 7. Event/sponsorship decision rule

A conference sponsorship is justified only if it materially improves access to qualified buyers.

Prefer packages that provide one or more of:

- speaking or moderated-demo opportunity;
- qualified attendee access / introductions where permitted;
- targeted email or member exposure;
- curated meetings;
- high-density networking;
- credible launch association with the organizer.

Avoid paying primarily for:

- logo placement;
- large generic booth footprints;
- broad consumer foot traffic;
- vanity impressions;
- events where fewer than ~20–30 plausible buyers are likely to attend.

### Initial one-time launch budget

Working planning range: **C$5,000–C$10,000 total**, subject to actual event packages.

Possible uses:

- selected event attendance/sponsorship;
- Ontario travel/accommodation;
- simple professional signage/display;
- concise printed leave-behind / QR demo asset;
- prospect-data/outreach tooling;
- modest launch-week LinkedIn/Google retargeting;
- follow-up/demo logistics.

Do not spend C$20K–C$30K on a large booth without evidence that it can directly produce the required first cohort.

# 8. Partners as distribution, not buyers

Contact relevant trade/economic-development organizations around launch, including where appropriate:

- Canadian Manufacturers & Exporters;
- Trade Commissioner Service / regional trade offices;
- Ontario trade/economic-development teams;
- local economic-development organizations;
- chambers and industry associations in selected Ontario clusters.

The ask is generally:

> **Can you share / introduce this tariff-exposure assessment with businesses in your network?**

Do not build special government workflows or institutional products to win these relationships.

# 9. Launch-week scorecard

The launch should have a hard commercial scorecard.

## Minimum acceptable

- 10 paying Business customers
- 1 paying Advisor customer

## Excellent

- 10 paying Business customers
- 3 paying Advisor customers

## Stretch

- 15–20 paying Business customers
- 3 paying Advisor customers

Track daily:

- booked demos;
- completed demos;
- qualified prospects;
- paid conversions;
- Business MRR;
- Advisor MRR;
- demo → paid conversion rate;
- time from demo → payment;
- primary objection;
- specific feature/output that caused purchase.

Do not judge the launch primarily by web traffic, social reach, leads collected or booth conversations.

# 10. Immediate 30-day post-launch plan

Once the launch campaign is complete, stop optimizing for launch theatre.

For the next 30 days:

1. personally interview every paying customer;
2. identify exactly why C$99 / C$249 felt worth paying;
3. fix product defects that block retention;
4. capture permissioned testimonials / case studies where appropriate;
5. ask for referrals to similar businesses / accounting firms;
6. convert the best-performing launch message into paid ads and founder-led outreach;
7. recycle available early cash into acquisition and product/data reliability;
8. push from the launch cohort toward **30 Business + 3 Advisor accounts**.

The launch is successful only if it creates a repeatable acquisition/retention learning loop.

---

# 11. What waits

Do not let the launch expand into:

- a national conference tour;
- recurring trade-show sponsorships;
- journalist/politician product builds;
- bank/institutional dashboards;
- broad influencer campaigns;
- expensive PR retainers;
- full video production before the web demos are proven;
- mass-market Meta advertising as the primary launch channel.

Journalists, politicians, lawyers and researchers may receive free/public access later as credibility/distribution users, but they are not the launch conversion target.

---

# Launch decision rule

Before approving any launch spend or activity, ask:

> **Will this materially increase the probability that we leave launch with at least 10 paying Business customers and 1 paying Advisor — and accelerate the path to 30 + 3?**

If not, do not do it.
