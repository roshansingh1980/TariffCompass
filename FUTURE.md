## Promotion rule

An item moves from `FUTURE.md` to `PENDING.md` only when one of the following is true:

1. Roshan explicitly promotes it;
2. real customer demand makes it necessary for conversion or retention; or
3. it becomes a dependency for one of the six C$99 launch/value gates.

Do not pull future work forward merely because it is technically interesting.
